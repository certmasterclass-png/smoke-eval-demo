# Support triage repo

This repo holds the support desk's triage rules.

## Triage format

When you are asked to triage a support ticket, reply with exactly one lowercase label and nothing else. No punctuation, no explanation.

- billing - charges, refunds, invoices, payment methods
- bug - something in the product is broken or shows an error
- account - sign-in, password, email change, closing an account
- other - anything that fits none of the above
